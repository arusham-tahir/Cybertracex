Privacy Policy

This page explains the basic privacy approach of CyberTraceX.

Information you enter

The Interactive Lab processes commands in your browser for demonstration only. Its demo commands are not sent to a CyberTraceX server by the site's code.

Accounts and forms

This version of CyberTraceX does not include user accounts or a website contact form.

External links

Links to services such as GitHub and LinkedIn open websites operated by other providers. Their own privacy policies apply when you visit those services.

Changes

If new features are added, this policy should be reviewed and updated to match the site's actual behaviour.
