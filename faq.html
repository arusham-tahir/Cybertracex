Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about CyberTraceX and basic cybersecurity concepts.

What is CyberTraceX?

CyberTraceX is an educational website that helps people understand cybersecurity and make safer choices online.

Is CyberTraceX a hacking tool?

No. It is designed for cybersecurity awareness and learning. Its Interactive Lab uses simulated examples rather than accessing or scanning real devices.

How can I protect myself from phishing?

Check the sender and website address carefully, avoid suspicious links, and never share passwords or verification codes with untrusted sources.

What is two-factor authentication?

Two-factor authentication adds another verification step to your sign-in process, helping protect your account if your password is compromised.
