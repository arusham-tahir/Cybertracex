BEGINNER GUIDES

Learn the basics

Short explanations of important cybersecurity concepts.

Phishing

Phishing is a deceptive message or website designed to trick someone into sharing information or opening unsafe content.

Stay safer: Check the sender and website address. Avoid unexpected links and never share one-time codes.

Multi-factor authentication (MFA)

MFA adds another verification step beyond a password, such as an authenticator app or security key.

Stay safer: Enable MFA on important accounts and never approve a sign-in you did not initiate.

Strong passwords

A long, unique password helps protect each account if another service is compromised.

Stay safer: Use a password manager and do not reuse the same password across websites.

Public Wi-Fi

Public networks may be misconfigured or monitored by other parties. A network name alone does not prove that a hotspot is genuine.

Stay safer: Use HTTPS websites, disable automatic connection, and avoid sensitive tasks on networks you do not trust.

Updates and patches

Updates can fix security weaknesses in operating systems, browsers and apps.

Stay safer: Install updates from official settings or trusted app stores.
